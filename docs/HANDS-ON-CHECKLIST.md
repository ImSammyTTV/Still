# Hands-on checklist

The things most likely to behave differently on a real machine than in Still's automated tests. Each takes a minute
or two. Note anything that feels slow, jumpy or confusing, not only what breaks.

Mark each one ✅ works, ⚠️ works but feels off (say how), or ❌ broken.

## Capture

1. **Area capture, twice in a row.** Take an area screenshot, then another straight away. The overlay should appear
   at once both times, with no flash of the old picture.
2. **Timer capture.** Start a capture with the 3-second timer. The countdown shows, Esc cancels it, and the
   screenshot taken at zero has no countdown in it.
3. **Move the Capture Bar.** Drag it by the Still icon, take a capture, then start a new one. It should be back in
   its usual place.
4. **Copy colour (C)** in the capture overlay. Paste somewhere: you should get the colour under the pointer.

## Editor

5. **Every tool once** (V A R F O L P H T N B U X S M D E C). Each key selects its tool, and Undo (Ctrl/⌘Z) undoes
   each mark.
6. **Hide Private Info** on a screenshot showing an email address and a card number. Both are covered and nothing
   else is.
7. **Save, close, reopen.** Edit a capture, close the editor, then open it again from History. The marks are still
   editable.

## Recording

8. **Record 30 seconds with microphone and computer sound.** Talk and play a video. Both sounds are in the file and
   in step with the picture.
9. **Pause, resume and stop from the shortcut** while a full-screen app is in front.
10. **Windows: Cursor effects on.** Record with Settings → Recording → Cursor effects. In the video editor, the
    cursor's Size, Style and Smooth settings change the export.

## Video editor

11. **Opening speed.** Open a recording of a few minutes. Note how long the window takes to become usable and the
    film strip to fill in.
12. **Cut, Fast-Forward (S) and Undo.** Cut a stretch (X at start and end), fast-forward another, then undo both.
    Each undo should say what it undid.
13. **Speed Up Waits** on a recording with a progress bar or a page loading. Only the waiting parts become
    fast-forwards.
14. **Find Chapters** on a recording that switches between apps or pages. Check the chapter names make sense.
15. **Export with everything.** Add a zoom, text, a blur, an arrow, music and a Fade opening, then export at 1080p.
    Play the file: everything is where it was in the preview, and the music dips when you speak.
16. **Export GIF** of a 10-second stretch. Its size and frame count match what the GIF sheet said.

## History and sharing

17. **A long History.** With 50+ captures, open History and scroll quickly. Nothing should stutter, and recordings
    should show their first frame as they come into view.
18. **A moved file.** Move one capture's file out of Still's folder in Explorer or Finder, then open History. Its
    tile says "File moved or deleted".
19. **Upload and copy link**, then open the link on your phone with **Show QR Code**.

## Things going wrong

20. **Disk nearly full.** Export a long video to a nearly full USB stick or drive. You should get "There isn't enough
    space on the disk", not a long technical error.
21. **Recording with no sound.** Use Tighten Pauses on a recording made without a microphone. It should simply say
    the recording has no sound.

## After the checklist

Send back the numbers marked ⚠️ or ❌ with a sentence each (and a screenshot where it helps). Those become the next
round of fixes.
