# Changelog

What changed in each version of Still. The newest is at the top. Each section becomes the notes of its release on
GitHub, so write for people using Still: what they'll notice, in plain words. Describe Still's own features only: never
name or compare with other apps or services.

## v1.2.53 — 2026-11-12

- **Adjust the area before capturing** (Mac, Windows, Linux). After you drag out an area, the box stays on screen. Move
  the pointer inside it and it becomes a hand: drag to move the box. Drag an edge or corner to resize it. Click
  **Capture** under the box, press Return, or double-click inside to take it; Esc cancels. Prefer capturing the moment
  you let go? Turn off Settings → Screenshots → **Adjust the area before capturing**.
- **Cloud upload removed** (Mac, Windows, Linux). Upload and Copy Link, automatic upload, share-link QR codes and the
  Still Cloud server are gone. To keep captures in the cloud, set Still's save folder to a folder your own cloud
  storage syncs. **Share…** still uses your computer's share menu.
- **Describe Image keys** now live in Settings → **AI**.
- **Mac:** Remember last selection is now off by default, as on Windows and Linux.

## v1.2.52 — 2026-11-12

- **Save for Size** (Mac, Windows, Linux). Save a capture square or as it is, at a chosen width, under a chosen file
  size, for example a 500 × 500 profile picture under 500 KB. Still lowers the quality first, then the size, until it
  fits. Find it on the Floating Preview, in History's right-click menu, and in the editor (⇧⌘E / Ctrl+Shift+E).
- **Move a selection while dragging** (Mac, Windows, Linux). Hold Space mid-drag to move the box without changing its
  size; let go of Space to keep resizing.

## v1.2.51 — 2026-11-11

- **Windows and Linux:** fixed the pointer and screen freezing when the capture screen opens, on large or
  high-resolution displays.

- **Annotate and polish** (Mac, Windows, Linux). Arrows, shapes, pen, highlighter, text, numbered steps, spotlight,
  blur and redact, all editable with undo, plus backgrounds with padding, rounded corners and shadows.
- **Quick Access** (Mac, Windows, Linux). A floating preview after every capture to copy, save, annotate, pin or drag
  it into another app. It closes after 15 seconds by default.
- **Record and copy text** (Mac, Windows, Linux). Record an area or the whole screen with microphone and computer
  sound, and copy text from any image or app; text recognition runs on your computer.
- **Pin, history and private sharing** (Mac, Windows, Linux). Pin captures above your windows, search past captures
  in Capture History, and optionally share through a Still Cloud server you run yourself.
