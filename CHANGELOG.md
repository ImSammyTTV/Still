# Changelog

What changed in each version of Still. The newest is at the top. Each section becomes the notes of its release on
GitHub, so write for people using Still: what they'll notice, in plain words.

## v1.2.53 — 2026-11-12

- **Adjust the area before capturing** (Mac, Windows, Linux). After you drag out an area, the box stays on screen. Move
  the pointer inside it and it becomes a hand: drag to move the box. Drag an edge or corner to resize it. Click
  **Capture** under the box, press Return, or double-click inside to take it; Esc cancels. Prefer capturing the moment
  you let go? Turn off Settings → Screenshots → **Adjust the area before capturing**.
- **Cloud upload removed** (Mac, Windows, Linux). Upload and Copy Link, automatic upload, share-link QR codes, the
  Still Cloud server, and Google Drive and iCloud Drive upload are gone. To keep captures in the cloud, set Still's save
  folder to your own iCloud Drive or Google Drive folder. **Share…** still uses your computer's share menu.
- **Describe Image keys** now live in Settings → **AI**.
- **Mac:** Remember last selection is now off by default, as on Windows and Linux.

## v1.2.52 — 2026-11-12

- **Save for Size** (Mac, Windows, Linux). Save a capture square or as it is, at a chosen width, under a chosen file
  size, for example a 500 × 500 profile picture under 500 KB. Still lowers the quality first, then the size, until it
  fits. Find it on the Floating Preview, in History's right-click menu, and in the editor (⇧⌘E / Ctrl+Shift+E).
- **Move a selection while dragging** (Mac, Windows, Linux). Hold Space mid-drag to move the box without changing its
  size; let go of Space to keep resizing.

## v1.2.51 — 2026-11-11

- **Windows and Linux:** fixed the pointer and screen freezing when the capture screen opens, on large or
  high-resolution displays.

## v1.2.50 — 2026-11-10

- **Mac, two displays:** the Capture Bar follows the pointer onto whichever display it's on, and **Screen** asks
  which display to capture or record. Record Screen's full-screen button lists each display by name.
- **Mac:** clicking Still in the Dock opens the Capture Bar. Right-click the Dock icon for Capture Bar, Record Screen,
  Capture History and Settings.

## v1.2.49 — 2026-11-09

- **Capture just the page inside a window** (Mac, Windows, Linux). Hold Space over a window and scroll down: the
  highlight moves from the whole window to the page inside it, without the tabs and toolbar. Scroll up for the whole
  window again.

Earlier versions are listed on the [releases page](https://github.com/ImSammyTTV/Still/releases).
